## Documented conflicts

### C-01 · Dashboard KPI periods

The general PRD (p.4 §5) shows current-month revenue/expenses; the Expo PRD (p.39 §6) shows YTD plus AR and upcoming bills.

**Resolution:** Followed the Expo MVP: YTD KPIs with AR and bills; current-month figures remain in the CFO view.

### C-02 · Reconciliation and Monthly Close placement

Detailed in the general PRD (§11, §14) but absent from the Expo module list and P0/P1 priorities.

**Resolution:** Built as core bookkeeping (classification A, priority 'Candidate') and placed under Finance as the brief's navigation specifies.

### C-03 · Forecasting scope

General PRD lists 'Advanced forecasting' and 'Budgeting' out of scope; Expo PRD makes the 13-week forecast and scenarios P0.

**Resolution:** 13-week forecast and basic scenarios built; budgeting and advanced FP&A remain out of scope.

### C-04 · Tax and R&D credits

Both PRD layers put tax filing and R&D credits out of scope; the brief asks for a tax workspace inspired by Fondo.

**Resolution:** Tax workspace built as a labelled Preview (classification B) that tracks deadlines, documents and approvals; filing and credit calculation remain C.

### C-05 · Positioning

General PRD: 'Your AI Finance Department' / AI-first bookkeeping. Expo PRD: 'AI Finance for Land & Agriculture — Books. Funding. CFO.'

**Resolution:** Expo positioning in-product; 'Your AI Finance Department' kept as the long-term vision.

### C-06 · Accounts receivable collection agents

General PRD lists 'Accounts-receivable collection agents' and 'Full AP automation' out of scope; Expo adds Collect Lite and Bills Lite.

**Resolution:** Built the Lite versions only: visibility, approvals and human-approved reminders — no automated dunning or payments.

### C-07 · Interactive prototype

The PRD links a claude.ai design prototype; it is not in the repository and could not be opened.

**Resolution:** Recorded as a limitation; the design follows the brief's design direction instead.

### C-08 · Technology stack

PRD suggests Next.js + Python/FastAPI + PostgreSQL + Redis + S3. Python and PostgreSQL were not available in the build environment.

**Resolution:** Implemented in Next.js/TypeScript with a repository boundary, provider adapters and a documented Postgres schema; see docs/DECISIONS.md.
